INSIDE THE DREAMING MIND

Why do we dream?

Every night, our minds create stories, images, and experiences that can feel surprisingly real. But why does this happen?

The mystery of dreaming

Scientists are still exploring why we dream. Some theories suggest that dreams may be connected to memory, emotions, and the way our brains process experiences.

Dreams can happen during different stages of sleep, although vivid dreams are especially common during REM sleep.

What are lucid dreams?

A lucid dream happens when someone becomes aware that they are dreaming while the dream is still taking place.

Some people report being able to influence what happens in their dreams, although this does not happen in every lucid dream.

Three interesting facts about dreams

01

Dreams are easily forgotten

Many dreams disappear from memory shortly after waking up.

02

Dreams involve emotions

Dreams can reflect feelings such as excitement, confusion, or fear.

03

We dream in different sleep stages

Dreaming is not limited to REM sleep, although dreams can differ between stages.

A QUESTION STILL UNANSWERED

Researchers have proposed several explanations for dreaming, but there is still no single scientific theory that explains every dream.
