THE ART OF REST

Small habits, better sleep.

Better sleep does not always require big changes. Small adjustments to our daily routines can make it easier to get the rest we need.

START WITH YOUR ENVIRONMENT

Make room for rest

A quiet, dark, and comfortably cool bedroom can help create the right conditions for sleep. Even simple changes, such as reducing noise or blocking unwanted light, may help.

Four habits worth trying

01

Keep a regular schedule

Try to go to bed and wake up at similar times each day, including weekends.

02

Get daylight during the day

Natural light helps regulate the body's internal clock and supports a healthy sleep-wake cycle.

03

Create a relaxing routine

Reading, listening to calm music, or doing another relaxing activity can help you wind down.

04

Limit late-night stimulation

Reduce bright light and stimulating activities close to bedtime when possible.

A GENTLER END TO THE DAY

A simple evening routine

An evening routine does not have to be complicated. Try setting aside a little time to slow down before going to bed.

  1. Dim the lights and put away distractions.
  2. Prepare anything you need for the next morning.
  3. Read a few pages of a book or relax quietly.
  4. Go to bed when you feel sleepy.
